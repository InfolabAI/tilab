# TILab — Trustworthy Intelligence Lab

**Website: [https://infolabai.github.io/lab_homepage/](https://infolabai.github.io/lab_homepage/)**

Professor and research lab website for Heechul Lim, Department of Artificial Intelligence, Tech University of Korea.

## Pages

- Home: research inquiries and lab news
- Research: research directions and projects
- Professor: profile, education, experience, and honors
- People: faculty, students, and alumni
- Publications: papers, dissertation, and patents
- Teaching: courses and invited talks

## Preview and update

Open `index.html` directly in a browser. No build step is required.

Edit the HTML pages and shared `styles.css`, then commit and push to `main`. GitHub Pages publishes from the repository root. The Contact button on Home leads to the Professor page.

Student and alumni profiles are currently placeholders. Local management notes and unused portraits are excluded from version control.
